### Reel script (15 seconds, vertical, phone)

Film in a real room on a real setup day, with the venue's permission. No guests' faces, no client names or logos legible on screen unless the client has said yes in writing. No AI footage. Shoot vertical, lock exposure, hold each shot still for 3 seconds or more so there is room to trim. Music: a quiet track from the Instagram library, low volume.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text (JetBrains Mono style, white box) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Wide, from the doorway: the empty gala room, chairs in, lights up | 6:00 | Phone at chest height, slow push in. Phone clock or a wall clock in frame if possible. |
| 0:03 to 0:06 | Medium: a set table, place cards and centrepiece | Tables set | Slide left to right along one table. |
| 0:06 to 0:10 | Close-up: the printed run of show taped to the AV table, a finger on the sponsor list | Sponsors' names in the run of show | Use a template page or blur the names. Keep a microphone or mixer edge in frame. |
| 0:10 to 0:13 | Close-up: a clock or phone lock screen reading 6:45 | 6:45 | Hold still. This is the beat. |
| 0:13 to 0:15 | From inside the room: the doors, closed, a hand reaching for the handle | The room is ready at 6:45. | End on the doors before they open. Last frame can hold the cover card for 1 second. |
